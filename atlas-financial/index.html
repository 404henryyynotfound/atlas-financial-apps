Our principles

Quiet tools. Serious clarity.

01

Clarity over noise

Financial software should reduce the number of questions you have, not add more dashboards. We design for the answer first.

02

Privacy by default

Your holdings are yours. Meridian keeps accounts and portfolio data in your browser, with export always available.

03

The whole portfolio

A fund here, a stock there, a little crypto somewhere else — real portfolios are messy. Our tools meet them as they are.

The company

Atlas Financial is a technology company, not a loud one.

We work at the intersection of market data, thoughtful interface design and personal finance. Our job is to turn a scattered financial life into something you can actually read — and then get out of the way.
