Who we are

A small idea with a big scope.

Most investing tools were designed for a simpler time: one brokerage, one account, one market. Real portfolios don't look like that. They hold funds from one provider, shares at another exchange, a little gold, a little crypto, and goals that have nothing to do with a benchmark.

Atlas Financial exists to build software for the financial lives people actually have. Meridian brings investments into one calm workspace; Airo makes everyday money and retirement readable; Tandem handles expenses we share with other people; Atlas Business brings every thread — including employee retirement plans — together for teams and owners; Atlas Calendar keeps every date visible through one account with personal, family, business and developer profiles.

We are a technology company. We do not manage money, place trades, or offer investment advice. Our work is the interface between you and your information.

The product

Meridian Finance

Meridian is an investing workspace for people who want every holding, every exchange and every headline in one field of view.

Every market. One clear view.

Build a portfolio across stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, bonds, commodities, real estate and digital assets. Search thousands of symbols, follow live quotes and history, watch the news tied to your positions, set goals, and read instant insights about concentration and performance.

Accounts are created locally with salted password derivation. Portfolio information lives in the browser — portable through JSON export and never sent to an Atlas server.

Approach

How we build.

01

Answer first

We start with the question a person is actually asking — “what changed today?”, “am I too concentrated?” — and design backwards from a clear answer.

02

Honest data

Live information is labelled as live. Cached or simulated fallbacks are labelled too. You should always know what a number represents.

03

Your information

Local accounts, local storage, portable exports. We believe trust is earned by restraint as much as by features.

Data & privacy

Market quotes, symbol search and historical prices in Meridian are supplied by Alpha Vantage. Headlines come from Google News. These providers receive only the requests needed to answer your query — never your portfolio. When a free-tier quota is exhausted or a feed is unavailable, Meridian falls back to cached or clearly-labelled sample data rather than leaving you with an empty screen.
