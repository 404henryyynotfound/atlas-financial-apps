The Meridian Connector

Separate tools. Connected when it helps.

Meridian generates a short-lived, single-use pairing code. Airo or Atlas Business redeems it and pulls a read-only snapshot of holdings, watchlist and goals. No passwords, broker logins or bank credentials are ever exchanged.

Start in Meridian ↗Link in Airo ↗

Connect in Meridian

Settings → Meridian Connector → Connect cloud account.

02
Copy the 6-digit code

It expires in 10 minutes and works once.

03
Redeem in Airo or Atlas Business

Open Connections → paste the Meridian code.

04
Stay live

Updates stream through Atlas Sync when reachable.

Your deadlines, where you already work.

Atlas Developers install

Three ways to install. One toolchain.

The CLI and GUI are published as npm packages, sudo-friendly system packages and portable release archives. Choose the method that fits your machine — every path verifies the same open, zero-dependency build.
